Read this first

This app turns public data into calibrated probabilities and compares them with bookmaker prices. It does not make guaranteed money. Bookmakers' prices already contain most of what a public model knows, and they keep a margin of about 5% on every market. Our own walk-forward backtest (see Track record) shows the model's "value bets" lost money against average prices; only shopping for the best available price came out slightly ahead, and even that result is not statistically certain.

Winning $1M a day would need tens of millions staked every day at a reliable edge. No public model has that, and bookmakers limit accounts that win. Use this as an analysis tool, size bets with the Kelly calculator, and never stake money you can't afford to lose.

How a pick is flagged

  1. The football model (Dixon-Coles) or tennis model (surface Elo) gives a probability.
  2. The market's own probability is taken from average odds with the margin removed.
  3. A selection is flagged only if the best price beats the market's fair price by your minimum edge and the model doesn't disagree. That was the only rule that didn't lose in testing.
  4. Stake = your Kelly fraction × full Kelly, capped at your maximum.

Responsible gambling

  • Only bet money you can afford to lose. Treat it as entertainment spend, not income.
  • Set a deposit and loss limit with your bookmaker before you start, and a time limit for each session.
  • Never chase losses by raising stakes. Losing streaks of 10-20 bets happen even with a real edge (see the backtest).
  • If betting stops being fun, take a break. Most bookmakers offer self-exclusion; GAMSTOP (UK), the National Council on Problem Gambling 1-800-GAMBLER (US) and BeGambleAware.org offer free, confidential help.
  • Betting is only legal for adults, and only where it is licensed in your country.
